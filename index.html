About Me.

Ph.D. researcher and materials scientist with 6+ years of experience in nanomaterials, photoelectrocatalysis, solar water splitting, biomass upgradation, and next-gen energy storage (supercapacitors). Proven track record in full-cycle R&D — from material design and device fabrication to electrochemical characterization, data analytics, and scale-up assessment. Published in leading journals and funded by multiple international fellowships and conference grants. Skilled in cross-functional collaboration, project leadership, scientific communication, and mentoring early-career researchers. Also active at the interface of science policy, IP strategy, and deep-tech entrepreneurship, supported by a PG Diploma in IPR and programs such as DST-GDC INCUBATE and BRICS Youth Energy. Dedicated to advancing climate-aligned technologies and circular-economy solutions through research industry policy integration. Open to global collaborations, clean-energy innovation roles, and partnerships across advanced materials, sustainability, and science-driven technology development.
